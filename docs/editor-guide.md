# Editor guide

A short how-to for editors of the Kingdom Capstone Outreach Ministries site. A copy with screenshots is shared separately; this file is the text source.

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the admin address your administrator gave you.
2. Enter your email address and password, then choose **Sign in**.
3. The menu on the left lists what you can edit: Dashboard, Capsules, Calendar, Homepage, Pages, Blog, Events, Books, Inbox and Subscribers. Users and Settings are for administrators only.
4. **Search content** at the top finds capsules, blog posts and events by title.

## 2. Write, schedule and publish a capsule

1. Choose **Capsules**, then **New capsule**.
2. Fill in the **Title** and the **Body**. Scripture reference, verse text, decrees, prayer, meditation, author and series are optional.
3. Choose **Save draft** at any time. A "Draft saved" message confirms it. Drafts are never public.
4. Check the **Publishing** card. **Publishes on** defaults to the next midnight, Nigerian time (Lagos). Pick a later date to write a few days ahead.
5. Choose **Schedule**. The capsule moves to Scheduled and goes live automatically at that time, within about a minute.
6. To publish immediately, open the **More actions** menu (the three dots) and choose **Publish now**.
7. To pull a capsule back, use **Unschedule** (scheduled) or **Unpublish** (live). Both return it to Draft.

The Capsules list shows each capsule's status and its Lagos date. The Calendar shows the same capsules by day.

## 3. Blog posts

1. Choose **Blog**, then new post.
2. Enter a **Title**. The **Slug** is the web address (`/blog/your-slug`); use lowercase words joined by hyphens.
3. Add an optional excerpt (the short summary shown in lists), tags and a cover image (PNG, JPEG or WebP).
4. Write the body with the toolbar: bold, italic, underline, headings, quote, lists, links and images.
5. Save, then publish when it is ready.

## 4. Events

1. Choose **Events**. Upcoming and Past events are listed on the left.
2. Select an event to edit it on the right, or choose **New event**.
3. Save, then publish when it is ready.

## 5. Homepage sections

1. Choose **Homepage**.
2. Drag a handle to reorder a section, or focus it and use the up and down arrow keys.
3. Switch a section off to hide it from the site. Today's Capsule always stays at the top.
4. Choose **Publish** to put the new layout live.

## 6. Unsaved changes

If you leave a page with edits you have not saved, a **Discard your changes?** box appears. Choose **Keep editing** to go back and save, or **Discard** to throw the edits away.

## 7. Staff training

Run a short session with each new editor:

1. Walk through sections 1 to 6 on the staging site.
2. Each editor creates, schedules and publishes a capsule unaided.
3. Note anything unclear and update this guide.

Done when at least two staff members have completed a capsule end to end on staging without help.
