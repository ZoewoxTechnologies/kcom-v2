KCOM Brand Directions

Three ways to refresh the colors and type around the existing logo. Each keeps the logo's blue as an anchor and aims for warm, pastoral, calm and prophetic, with a different emphasis.

Chosen base · Still Waters, refined with gold

Still Waters, with Old Gold

Direction B's calm blues and sage, with a muted old gold for small accents and a soft sandstone for highlighted blocks. Each kind of text gets its own role.

Palette
Deep water
Sage
Old gold
Sandstone
Mist

Why old gold: it sits between Watchman's flame gold and Morning Light's sunrise, desaturated so it stays calm next to the blues. It's used sparingly: scripture rules, references, the decree marker, focus rings.

Why sandstone: a warm wash for the decree and highlighted blocks, softer than gold as a background. Dawn was close, but slightly too peach beside sage.

Text in gold uses a darker shade so it stays readable.

Buttons
Primary: the one main action on a screen Secondary: other actions beside it Tertiary: looks like a link; low-emphasis actions Giving (special case): gold primary, used only for giving, later milestone
