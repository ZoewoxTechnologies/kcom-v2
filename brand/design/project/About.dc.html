ABOUT KCOM Who We Are Team and Arms

KCOM is a prophetic, teaching and intercessory outreach with a mandate to encourage, equip and motivate individuals and churches to operate in the realms of grace and glory, from the divine platforms of power, purity and perfection in Christ.

Inspired by Matthew 6:10, KCOM seeks to touch and transform lives with the prophetic messages of revelation, restoration and revival.
