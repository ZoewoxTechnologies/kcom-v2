The Renewed Mind · Day 11 of 12

Be Ye Transformed

BIBLE MEDITATION

And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

Romans 12:2 · KJV

Every day the world presses on us, quietly and constantly, asking us to take its shape. Its fears, its hurry and its definition of success all arrive without invitation. Paul does not tell the believers in Rome to escape the world, but to refuse its mould.

Transformation, he says, begins in the mind. Not by trying harder, but by letting the Word of God renew the way we think.

So today, before the noise of the day settles in, sit with one verse. Read it slowly, and receive His truth.

Decrees

✦I decree that my mind is renewed daily by the Word of God.

✦I refuse to be conformed to the pattern of this world, in Jesus’ name.

✦My life proves the good, acceptable and perfect will of God.

Optional: this section appears only when the capsule has decrees.

Prayer

Father, thank You for Your Word that renews my mind. Reshape me by Your Spirit, and let my life show Your good and perfect will. In Jesus’ name, amen.
