Thoughts Taken Captive

BIBLE MEDITATION

Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ.

2 Corinthians 10:5 · KJV

Not every thought that visits us deserves a place to stay. Paul speaks the language of a battlefield: strongholds, imaginations, captives. The struggle he describes is not first in our circumstances but in our minds.

To take a thought captive is to stop it at the gate and ask whose voice it carries. Does it agree with what God has said? If not, we do not argue with it endlessly; we bring it to Christ and let His Word have the final say.

Decrees

✦Every imagination that exalts itself against God in my mind is cast down.

✦My thoughts are obedient to Christ.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, I bring every thought to You today. Guard my mind with Your truth. Amen.
