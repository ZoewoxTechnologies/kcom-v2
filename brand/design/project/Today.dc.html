Be Ye Transformed

BIBLE MEDITATION

And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

Romans 12:2 · KJV

Every day the world presses on us, quietly and constantly, asking us to take its shape. Its fears, its hurry and its definition of success all arrive without invitation. Paul does not tell the believers in Rome to escape the world, but to refuse its mould.

Transformation, he says, begins in the mind. Not by trying harder, but by letting the Word of God renew the way we think. As His thoughts become our thoughts, our choices follow, and our lives begin to prove, to show plainly, what the will of God is: good, acceptable and perfect.

So today, before the noise of the day settles in, sit with one verse. Read it slowly. Ask the Holy Spirit to show you one place where the world has shaped your thinking, and receive His truth there instead.

The world asks for your shape; the Word gives you His.

Decrees

  • ✦I decree that my mind is renewed daily by the Word of God.
  • ✦I refuse to be conformed to the pattern of this world, in Jesus’ name.
  • ✦My life proves the good, acceptable and perfect will of God.
Optional: this section appears only when the capsule has decrees.

Prayer

Father, thank You for Your Word that renews my mind. Where I have taken the shape of this world, reshape me by Your Spirit. Let my thoughts, choices and words today show Your good and perfect will. In Jesus’ name, amen.
